The River Box

A little box by the river with a simple idea: take something, leave something, or just stop for a while.

What is the River Box?

The River Box is a small community space by the river.

It's a place where people can find little things to enjoy, leave something behind for someone else, write in the log book, make something, or simply sit by the water for a bit.

There's no complicated rule to it. The whole idea is just to create a small place where people can connect, share and enjoy something simple.

How it works

🎁

Take something

If there's something in the box you'd like, take it. It's there to be enjoyed.

💙

Leave something

Have something small you'd like to pass along? You can leave it for the next person.

✍️

Write something

The log book gives people a place to leave a name, a thought, a drawing or a little message.

🌿

Just be there

You don't have to take anything or leave anything. You can simply enjoy the river and take a little time for yourself.

What might you find?

What's in the River Box can change. That's part of what makes it interesting.

🪨

Little Treasures

Small rocks, keepsakes and other little things someone might enjoy finding.

⭐

Paper Stars

Little handmade pieces that can be taken, left behind or simply enjoyed.

🐟

Creative Things

Handmade ideas and simple activities that give people something fun to do.

📖

The Log Book

A place for visitors to leave their name, a thought, a drawing or a little piece of their visit.

A little reminder

The River Box belongs to the community.

Keeping it clean, respectful and welcoming helps make sure other people can enjoy it too.

Take what you need. Leave what you can. And please leave the space better than you found it. 🌊

Starting small

The River Box is one small part of The Ripple Effect Project.

It's not meant to be perfect, and it's still growing. For now, it's simply a little place by the river where kindness, creativity and community can meet.

Maybe someone finds something they needed. Maybe someone leaves something that makes another person smile. Maybe someone just sits by the water for ten minutes and feels a little better than when they arrived.

We don't always get to see where a ripple ends. We just get to start one.
