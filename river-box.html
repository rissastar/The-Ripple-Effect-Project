The River Box

A little box by the river where kindness, creativity and community can come together.

What is the River Box?

The River Box is a small community project built around one simple idea: sometimes people just need a little something to make their day better.

It's a place where you can take something, leave something, write something, create something, or simply stop for a minute and enjoy where you are.

It doesn't have to be a big gesture. That's kind of the point.

How it works

🎁

Take something

If there's something inside that makes you smile or brightens your day, take it.

💛

Leave something

Have something small you'd like to pass along? You can leave a little kindness for someone else.

📝

Write something

The log book gives people a place to leave a thought, a message, a drawing, or simply their name.

🌿

Just be there

You don't have to take or leave anything. Sometimes sitting by the river and taking a breath is enough.

What might you find?

The River Box isn't meant to have the exact same things every time. What's inside can change as people contribute and as new ideas are added.

🪨

Little treasures

Small rocks, keepsakes and other little things meant to be discovered.

⭐

Paper stars

Tiny reminders that even something small can brighten someone's day.

🐟

Creative things

Ideas like little wooden fish that people can colour and make their own.

📖

The log book

A place for people to leave their mark and see that other people have been there too.

A little reminder

The River Box belongs to the community. Please help keep the space clean, respectful and welcoming so the next person can enjoy it too.

This project is still small and growing. There will be changes, new ideas and probably a few things we learn along the way.

That's okay. The whole idea behind The Ripple Effect Project is starting small and seeing where that kindness can go.

One small act. One person. One ripple at a time. 🌊
