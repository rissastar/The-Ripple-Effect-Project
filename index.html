💙 What is it?

The Ripple Effect Project is a community kindness project based in East Hants, Nova Scotia.

The idea is simple: one small act of kindness can create another, and another, and another.

Sometimes people don't need something huge. They just need a reminder that someone noticed them, cared about them, or wanted to make their day a little easier.

🌱 What we're doing

We're starting small and focusing on the things we can do right now.

🎒

Kindness Bags

Small bags put together with useful and comforting items for people who may need a little extra kindness.

📦

The River Box

A little community box by the river where people can take something, leave something, write in the book, or simply stop for a moment.

🌊 That's the ripple.

One person helps another. Then that person helps someone else. Eventually, one little act doesn't feel so little anymore.

📍 Our Community

The Ripple Effect Project is based around East Hants, Nova Scotia, with kindness reaching into surrounding communities, including areas between Truro and Bedford.

We're starting here and seeing where the ripples take us.
