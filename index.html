The Ripple Effect Project

Small acts of kindness can travel further than we ever see.

What is it?

The Ripple Effect Project is about doing small things that can make someone else's day a little easier, a little lighter, or just a little better.

It started with a simple idea: one small act can reach someone, and that person might carry it forward to someone else.

You never really know how far a little kindness can travel.

What we're doing

Right now, the project is starting small with two things: Kindness Bags and the River Box.

💙

Kindness Bags

Small bags put together with useful things, personal care items, snacks, and little reminders that someone cares.

Learn More
🌊

The River Box

A little community space by the river where people can take something, leave something, write something, create something, or simply stop and enjoy the moment.

Learn More

That's the ripple.

Maybe you give someone a Kindness Bag.

Maybe someone finds a little note at the River Box when they really needed one.

Maybe they smile. Maybe they feel seen. Maybe they go home and do something kind for somebody else.

We may never see every part of the ripple. That's okay.

The point is to start it.

Our community

The Ripple Effect Project is based around East Hants, Nova Scotia, with the hope of reaching surrounding communities as it grows.

From Truro to Bedford and in between, the idea is simple: kindness doesn't need a big budget, a big organization, or a perfect plan to matter.

Want to help?

There are lots of ways to be part of the ripple. You don't have to donate money.

You can donate supplies, share the project, make something, leave a little kindness somewhere, or simply pass the idea along.
