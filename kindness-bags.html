Kindness Bags

A small bag can carry more than just supplies. It can carry a reminder that someone cares.

💙 What are Kindness Bags?

Kindness Bags are small bags put together with useful and comforting items for people who may need a little extra kindness.

They're not meant to solve someone's problems or change their whole situation. They're simply a small way of saying: you matter.

🎒 What's inside?

The contents can change depending on what is available and what people may need.

🧼 Personal care items

Simple everyday essentials.

🍎 Small snacks

Something useful and comforting.

🧦 Practical items

Small things that can make a day a little easier.

💌 A little kindness

A reminder that someone thought about them.

🌱 Why small things matter

We don't always know what someone is going through.

A small bag might seem like a small gesture, but for someone having a difficult day, knowing that somebody cared enough to put it together can mean something.

That's where the ripple begins.

🤝 Want to help?

The easiest way to help the Kindness Bags project is by supporting the supplies that go into them.

  • Donate useful supplies
  • Donate toward supplies
  • Share the project
  • Tell someone about the project
