🌊 The Ripple Effect Project

Small acts of kindness can create ripples far beyond where they begin.

⸻

💙 What is it?

The Ripple Effect Project is a community kindness project based in East Hants, Nova Scotia.

The idea is simple:

Do something kind.
Help someone feel seen.
Start a ripple.

⸻

🎒 What we’re doing

Kindness Bags

Small bags filled with useful, comforting, and thoughtful items for people who might need a little extra kindness.

📦 The River Box

A small community box by the river where people can:

* Take something
* Leave something
* Write in the book
* Create something
* Stop for a moment

It’s a little place for kindness, connection, and community.

⸻

📍 Where we’re going

We’re starting in East Hants and reaching into surrounding communities, including areas between Truro and Bedford.

We’re keeping it small for now and letting it grow naturally.

⸻

🌱 The idea behind it

You never really know what someone else is carrying.

A small gesture might seem insignificant to you, but it could mean something completely different to the person receiving it.

One person helps another.
That person helps someone else.
And the ripple keeps going.

⸻

🤝 How you can help

You don’t have to do anything huge.

You can:

* 🎁 Donate supplies
* 💵 Support the project
* 📣 Share the project
* 📦 Contribute to the River Box
* 💙 Do something kind for someone
* 🌊 Start your own ripple

⸻

🚀 What’s next?

The project is still growing.

There are ideas for where it could go in the future, but we’re taking it one step at a time.

Right now, we’re focusing on:

Kindness Bags + The River Box + creating little moments of kindness.

⸻

One small act. One person. One ripple at a time. 💙
