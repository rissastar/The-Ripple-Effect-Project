Have an idea?

The Ripple Effect Project is starting small, but that doesn't mean the ideas have to.

If you've got an idea for something kind, creative, useful, or just something that could brighten someone's day, we'd love to hear it.

Sometimes an idea starts with one person simply saying, "What if we tried this?"

That's how ripples start.

Where we're starting

The Ripple Effect Project is based around East Hants, Nova Scotia, with the hope of reaching surrounding communities as it grows.

From Truro to Bedford and in between, the goal is to keep things simple, genuine, and focused on small acts that can make a difference.

One small act. One person. One ripple at a time. 🌊
