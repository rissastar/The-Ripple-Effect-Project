Have an idea?

The project is growing slowly, and not every idea has to become a full project right away.

Sometimes an idea starts with one person saying, "What if we tried this?"

So if you have an idea for spreading a little more kindness in the community, we'd love to hear it.

Where we're starting

The Ripple Effect Project is based around East Hants, Nova Scotia, with the hope of reaching surrounding communities as the project grows.

For now, we're keeping things manageable and focusing on what we can actually do. One small ripple at a time.

One small act. One person. One ripple at a time. 🌊
