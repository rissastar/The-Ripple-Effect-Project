How You Can Help

You don't have to do something huge to make a difference. Sometimes the smallest things are what matter most.

There are lots of ways to be part of the ripple.

The Ripple Effect Project is starting small with Kindness Bags and the River Box. As the project grows, there are lots of ways people can help keep that kindness moving.

And you don't need to have a lot of money, supplies, or time. Kindness can be simple.

🎒

Donate Supplies

Help fill Kindness Bags with useful items, personal care products, snacks, or other little things that someone might need.

💙

Make a Donation

Financial donations can help purchase supplies for Kindness Bags and things for the River Box.

Support the Project
🎨

Share Your Creativity

Make something small that could brighten someone's day, whether it's a note, a little drawing, or something creative for the River Box.

📣

Spread the Word

Share the project with someone else. Sometimes simply telling another person about an idea is enough to start another ripple.

Donations help, but kindness doesn't have to cost anything.

You can hold a door open for someone. Check on somebody. Leave a kind note. Pick up something that isn't yours. Let someone know they're not invisible.

The Ripple Effect Project isn't about how much you give. It's about choosing to give something when you can.

You never know what someone is carrying when you pass them.

A small act of kindness might mean more than you'll ever know.

Starting small is still starting.

Right now, we're focusing on Kindness Bags and the River Box. That's where the ripple begins.

As the project grows, we'll keep looking for simple ways to bring more kindness into the community.

One small act. One person. One ripple at a time. 🌊
