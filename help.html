How You Can Help

It doesn't take a lot to be part of a ripple. Sometimes the smallest things are the ones that reach someone.

Want to be part of it?

The Ripple Effect Project is still small, and we're building it one step at a time.

Right now, we're focused on Kindness Bags and The River Box. Support helps us keep those things going and gives us the chance to slowly grow the project.

There are lots of ways to help

🛍️

Donate supplies

Small items can help fill Kindness Bags or add something special to The River Box.

💰

Make a donation

Financial donations can help cover the cost of supplies and keep the project moving forward.

🎨

Share your creativity

Handmade notes, drawings, little crafts and simple creative ideas can become part of the project.

📣

Spread the word

Sharing the project with someone else is a form of support too. You never know who might want to be part of it.

Donations

Every little bit helps.

Donations go toward supplies for the Kindness Bags, The River Box and the little things that help bring the project to life.

Support the Project

We want to keep things simple and honest. The project is growing gradually, and we're not pretending to have everything figured out. We're just starting with what we can do and seeing where the ripple goes.

You don't have to donate

Being part of the Ripple Effect doesn't have to cost anything.

Be kind to someone. Leave an encouraging note. Pick up a piece of garbage. Check on someone. Share something you have. Make somebody laugh. Let someone know they're not invisible.

That's the whole idea.

One small act. One person. One ripple at a time. 🌊
