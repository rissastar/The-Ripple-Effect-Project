About the Project

It started with a simple idea: what if one small act of kindness could become something bigger?

Why the Ripple Effect?

Sometimes the smallest things can mean more than we realize.

A kind word. A little bag of things someone needs. Something left behind for the next person. A reminder that somebody thought about them.

We don't always get to see what happens after that moment. But that doesn't mean it didn't matter.

That's where the idea for The Ripple Effect Project came from.

Starting Small

This project isn't starting with a huge organization or a big budget. It's starting small and growing from there.

Right now, we're focusing on two simple things: Kindness Bags and the River Box.

💙

Kindness Bags

Small bags filled with useful things, personal care items, snacks and little reminders of kindness.

🌊

The River Box

A small community space by the river where people can take, leave, write, create or simply pause for a while.

How a Ripple Works

The idea is pretty simple.

1️⃣

One small act

Someone does something kind, even if it seems small.

2️⃣

Someone receives it

That kindness reaches another person at a moment when they might need it.

3️⃣

It keeps moving

Maybe that person carries the feeling with them or passes a little kindness along.

4️⃣

The ripple grows

One moment becomes another, and another. We may never know how far it reaches.

Our Community

The Ripple Effect Project is based around East Hants, Nova Scotia, with surrounding communities in mind as the project grows.

From Truro to Bedford and in between, we're starting where we are and seeing where the ripple goes.

This project is still small. That's part of the point. It doesn't have to be huge to matter.

Where we're going

For now, we're taking things one step at a time.

The hope is that The Ripple Effect Project can continue growing naturally as more people discover it and want to be part of it.

No giant plan. No pretending we have everything figured out. Just starting with kindness and seeing where it takes us.

One small act. One person. One ripple at a time. 🌊
