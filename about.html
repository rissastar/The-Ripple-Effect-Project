About the Project

A small idea built around something simple: kindness doesn't have to be big to matter.

💙 Why the Ripple Effect?

The Ripple Effect Project was created around the belief that small acts of kindness can reach further than we ever get to see.

We don't always know what someone is going through. A person might be having one of the hardest days of their life and still look completely fine from the outside.

Sometimes a small act can be enough to remind someone that they aren't invisible.

You never really know how far one small act of kindness might travel.

🌱 Starting Small

The Ripple Effect Project isn't about trying to change everything overnight.

We're starting with what we can actually do right now: Kindness Bags and the River Box.

From there, we'll see where the project goes.

🌊 How a Ripple Works

1. Someone does something kind. Maybe it's something as simple as giving someone something they need.
2. Someone feels seen. That moment might mean more than the person who gave it ever knows.
3. Kindness gets passed on. Sometimes the person who received kindness ends up giving some to someone else.
4. The ripple grows. One small action becomes part of something much bigger.

📍 Our Community

We're based around East Hants, Nova Scotia, and are reaching into surrounding communities, including areas between Truro and Bedford.

We're keeping the project grounded in the community and growing one step at a time.

🌊 It starts somewhere.

For us, it starts with one small act.

One person. One act. One ripple at a time.
